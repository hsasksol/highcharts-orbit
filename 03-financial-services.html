Why has our portfolio suddenly become more volatile?

An investment manager monitors the performance and risk of a diversified portfolio. Overall portfolio value remains relatively stable, but volatility has increased sharply. Use the Orbit toolbar on each chart to investigate what is driving the change.

The investigation

  1. Anomaly detection: on the volatility chart, Orbit flags an unusual increase in daily portfolio volatility even though portfolio value is relatively stable.
  2. Compare with benchmark: the portfolio shows substantially larger recent swings than the broader market.
  3. Filter / focus: Technology holdings account for a disproportionate share of the swings.
  4. Correlations: the technology holdings that previously behaved differently now move together.
  5. Indicators: apply a moving average (SMA) to volatility to see the elevated level persisting.
