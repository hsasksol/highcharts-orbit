Why has our portfolio suddenly become more volatile?

An investment manager monitors the performance and risk of a diversified portfolio. Overall portfolio value remains relatively stable, but volatility has increased sharply. Use the Orbit toolbar on each chart to investigate what is driving the change.

How to use Orbit

Step 1 of 5

Anomaly detection

Daily portfolio volatility jumps from about 8% to 19% with spikes at 24.8% and 26.3%, even though portfolio value holds up.

How: Open Analyze in the chart toolbar, then Quality, and run Anomaly detection.

Step 2 of 5

Compare

The portfolio swings far more than the benchmark in the last 30 days; put the two side by side.

How: Click a value in any chart to filter the whole page to it, or open Compare in the top bar to put two segments side by side.

Step 3 of 5

Filter and Focus

Technology contributes 62% of the variance from a 28% weight; the treemap makes the imbalance visible.

How: Open Transform in the chart toolbar, then Filter and Focus, and narrow to the series you care about.

Step 4 of 5

Correlations

The three technology holdings that used to follow different paths now move nearly in lockstep.

How: Open Analyze in the chart toolbar, then Relationships, and run Correlations across the series.

Step 5 of 5

Indicators

On the candle chart, Bollinger Bands widen sharply in the last month: the visual signature of the new regime.

How: Use the Indicators panel on the candle chart to add an SMA or Bollinger Bands.
