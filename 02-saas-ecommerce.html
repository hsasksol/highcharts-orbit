Why did my online sales suddenly drop?

An e-commerce analytics platform gives merchants a dashboard for monitoring their stores. This merchant sees a sudden drop in sales despite normal website traffic. Use the Orbit toolbar on each chart to investigate what changed.

The Orbit toolbar did not load. Serve this page from a domain on the Orbit installation's referrer allowlist; opened from a local file the charts degrade silently to plain Highcharts.

How to use Orbit on this demo

Step 1 of 7

Insights

Daily sales fall about 18% over the last two weeks while traffic stays steady; the problem is conversion, not demand.

How: Open the AI menu in the chart toolbar and choose Insights; Orbit writes up what changed and when.

Step 2 of 7

Compare

The mobile band in the stacked chart carries the whole decline; desktop is unchanged.

How: Click a value in any chart to filter the whole page to it, or open Compare in the top bar to put two segments side by side.

Step 3 of 7

Filter and Focus

Within mobile, the Paid Search stream thins from about 11k to under 5k USD; the other sources keep their width.

How: Open Transform in the chart toolbar, then Filter and Focus, and narrow to the series you care about.

Step 4 of 7

Anomaly detection

Cart abandonment for Mobile plus Paid Search visitors spikes from 68% to over 80% in the same period.

How: Open Analyze in the chart toolbar, then Quality, and run Anomaly detection.

Step 5 of 7

Correlations

As abandonment rises, conversion rate falls; the two series mirror each other.

How: Open Analyze in the chart toolbar, then Relationships, and run Correlations across the series.

Step 6 of 7

Distribution

The histogram of daily sales shows two separated clusters: a regime change, not gradual noise.

How: Open View in the chart toolbar and switch to the histogram view, or use the Distribution tool.

Step 7 of 7

Forecast

Project sales forward to estimate the impact if the trend continues unaddressed.

How: Open Analyze in the chart toolbar, then Trends, and project the series forward.
