Why did my online sales suddenly drop?

An e-commerce analytics platform gives merchants a dashboard for monitoring their stores. This merchant sees a sudden drop in sales despite normal website traffic. Use the Orbit toolbar on each chart to investigate what changed.

The Orbit toolbar did not load. Serve this page from a domain on the Orbit installation's referrer allowlist; opened from a local file the charts degrade silently to plain Highcharts.

The investigation

  1. Insights: on the sales chart, let Orbit highlight the two-week period where sales fall about 18% while traffic stays steady.
  2. Compare devices: mobile sales decline sharply while desktop stays normal.
  3. Filter / focus: within mobile, Paid Search accounts for most of the decline.
  4. Anomaly detection: cart abandonment for Mobile + Paid Search visitors spikes in the same period.
  5. Correlations: as abandonment rises, conversion rate falls.
  6. Forecast: project sales forward to estimate the impact if the trend continues.
