Is this machine starting to fail?

A manufacturing plant monitors sensor data from equipment on a production line. Machine 7 is still operating normally, but its sensor readings have begun to change. Use the Orbit toolbar on each chart to judge whether this is an early warning.

The investigation

  1. Control limits: apply control limits to Machine 7's vibration; readings approach and then exceed its normal operating range.
  2. Anomaly detection: Orbit flags an unusual pattern of vibration spikes not present in the other machines.
  3. Compare machines: Machines 5, 6 and 8 run under similar loads and stay within range.
  4. Correlations: as vibration increases, temperature and power consumption rise as well.
  5. Forecast: project the vibration trend forward; readings move further outside the limits.
  6. Annotate: mark the drift onset and note the similarity to the period before the previous maintenance event.
