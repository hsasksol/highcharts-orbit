Why are orders suddenly taking longer to fulfill?

A US-based operations dashboard shows that average order fulfillment time has recently increased, while overall order volume remains normal. Use the Orbit toolbar on each chart to find the source of the slowdown.

How to use Orbit

Step 1 of 5

Insights

On the fulfillment chart, average time climbs from about 1.8 to 2.6 days while order volume stays flat. Let Orbit highlight and explain the change.

How: Open the AI menu in the chart toolbar and choose Insights; Orbit writes up what changed and when.

Step 2 of 5

Compare

The East Coast warehouse accounts for most of the increase; Midwest and West stay near their 1.8-day baseline.

How: Click a value in any chart to filter the whole page to it, or open Compare in the top bar to put two segments side by side.

Step 3 of 5

Filter and Focus

Within East Coast, Furniture is the category taking far longer; Electronics and Apparel are flat.

How: Open Transform in the chart toolbar, then Filter and Focus, and narrow to the series you care about.

Step 4 of 5

Correlations

Late shipments and customer complaints rise together in the same weeks; one drives the other.

How: Open Analyze in the chart toolbar, then Relationships, and run Correlations across the series.

Step 5 of 5

Contribution

One carrier holds about 60% of the late shipments; the donut shows each carrier’s share of the problem.

How: Open the Contribution tool from the chart toolbar to rank how much each part makes up of the whole.
